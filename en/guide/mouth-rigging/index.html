Which mouth rigging option fits your needs?

For basic conversation, start with the Basic LD 9-point sample. For more detailed speech and emotional expression, compare the Premium LD 12-point sample. Discuss VBridger support and options such as mouth X, cheek puff and tongue-out separately.

These samples are not a controlled performance test. Results depend on the character design and separated parts, so point counts alone do not establish superior quality. This page is not a VBridger installation or connection tutorial.
