Check what to prepare before commissioning

  • A PSD with separated parts
  • Your display name and the illustrator’s name
  • Intended model use and debut or usage date
  • Requested expressions, props, outfits and hairstyle toggles
  • Shareable links to reference images and videos
  • Requests to delay portfolio publication or keep work off stream

How separated should the PSD be?

The face, facial features, hair and body need to be separated. Face fills, lines and shadows, as well as eyes, nose, mouth and eyebrows, should be separated for rigging. The final feasible scope is confirmed after reviewing the PSD.

What if some parts are not separated?

If most parts are ready and only minor additional separation is needed, work may proceed for an additional fee. A largely unseparated file is difficult to work with. Additional part separation requires permission from the illustrator.

List your expression, outfit, hair and mouth options

List expressions, props, outfits and hairstyle toggles separately, with samples that show your preferred direction. Specify advanced speech options such as iOS, VBridger or mouth X before payment.

How to share files and references for a quote

A full-body illustration can support a provisional estimate, but a detailed quote requires PSD review. This website’s inquiry form has no file-upload field: enter shareable PSD and reference links in the reference-links field, and check that the recipient can access them.

When applying through ArtMug, follow that channel’s order form and file-sharing instructions. Check which channel you are using rather than treating the website’s link-based inquiry and the ArtMug application as the same process.

Include your schedule and publication preferences

Include your debut or usage date and planned publication date. Scheduling is confirmed in payment order. LD work usually takes about 2–4 weeks and SD work about 1–2 weeks, depending on design and additional scope.

Finished work and work in progress may appear in portfolios, social media and YouTube. Mention any request to keep work off stream or delay publication before debut. Current guidance allows work to remain unpublished for up to two months at no extra charge; fees may apply afterward.
