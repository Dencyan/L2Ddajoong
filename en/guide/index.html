Before Commission

What to Prepare Before Contacting

Illustration work is not included, so a fully separated PSD is required. Please describe your requirements in as much detail as possible before payment.

  • A fully separated PSD file
  • Your name and the illustrator's name
  • Debut date or intended use date
  • Model use and planned reveal date
  • Requested expressions, props, outfits, and hairstyle toggles
  • Whether you need iOS, VBridger, Mouth X, or other advanced mouth options
  • Any request to restrict streaming or delay portfolio publication

Workflow

9 Steps from Inquiry to Delivery

The schedule is confirmed in payment order, not inquiry order. A standard model usually takes about 2 to 4 weeks.

  1. ContactSend your request and basic information.
  2. File Review and QuoteThe PSD structure and requirements are reviewed before a quote is provided.
  3. Booking and PaymentWork begins after the schedule is confirmed.
  4. First Rigging PassThe head and face structure is built.
  5. First ReviewFeedback is applied after you review the video.
  6. Second Rigging PassBody, props, and physics details are completed.
  7. Second ReviewFinal motion is reviewed and revised.
  8. Completion and DeliveryThe final files are delivered.
  9. Post-installation FixesIssues found while applying the files are checked.

Review and Revision Scope

Standard revisions are handled during two review rounds. Rigging errors found after installation are fixed free of charge.

Commission Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions Before Commissioning

Here are the preparation, schedule, revision, and publication details that are most often checked before a rigging commission. Items that vary heavily by model, such as advanced options or pricing details, are confirmed after reviewing the PSD.

The face, facial features, hair, and body should be separated into usable parts. The face should include separate areas such as surfaces, lines, and shadows, and key features such as eyes, nose, mouth, and eyebrows should be separated for rigging.

It depends on the file. If most parts are prepared and only minimal additional separation is needed, work may proceed with an extra fee. If most parts are not separated, the commission may not be possible. Additional part separation requires permission from the illustrator.

LD models usually take about 2 to 4 weeks, and SD models usually take about 1 to 2 weeks. The schedule may extend depending on the model design, additional expressions, outfits, hairstyles, and overall scope.

Two review rounds are included during the work period, with one revision round for each review. One final check is also included after delivery for rigging errors found in the delivered model files. Excessive revision requests or repeated reversals may incur additional fees.

References are welcome, but they are used only as direction. Each character suits different motion and physics, so the result will not be copied exactly from the reference.

Please attach the PSD file for an accurate quote. A full-body illustration can be used for a temporary quote, but a detailed quote is provided after checking the PSD.

Completed models and work-in-progress material may be posted on portfolios, social media, and YouTube. Private publication before debut is available for up to two months at no additional cost; additional fees may apply after that.

Reselling, transferring, sharing model data, or claiming the work as your own is prohibited. The model may be used for livestreaming and YouTube, while merchandise use requires permission from the illustrator.
